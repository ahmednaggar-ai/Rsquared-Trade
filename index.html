Italy

Crusher buckets & attachments

For over 20 years MB has been offering the largest line of patented crusher buckets and accessories for excavators, skid loaders and backhoes of all sizes, with extensive innovation and development.

The values of the family-owned business have enabled MB Crusher to reach internationally recognised excellence. Founded and headquartered in Italy, it operates globally through 7 international subsidiaries with logistic centres on different continents, plus an extensive network of authorised dealers and service support.

Explore MB Crusher
Germany

Asphalt batch-mix & premix plants

Environmentally friendly Lintec and Linnhoff asphalt batch mix plants and asphalt premix plants reduce your carbon footprint, lower the overall energy consumed during mixing operations, and allow you to recycle RAP material.

These plants produce hot mix and warm mix asphalt for road and bridge surfacing, tunnels and airport runways.

Explore Lintec & Linnhoff
Germany

Paving & hardscape machinery

Optimas is the leading global manufacturer of machines and equipment for paving job sites and is committed to empowering people to accomplish more.

Optimas employs more than 80 people at its factory in Saterland-Ramsloh, in north-west Germany between the Dutch border and Bremen, and works with sales and service partners all around the world.

Explore Optimas
Türkiye

Construction machinery

HİDROMEK, a globally known manufacturer of construction machinery, was founded in Ankara in 1978. Its founder, Hasan Basri Bozkurt, started by producing attachments for agricultural tractors; in the following years the company went on to produce the first construction machine ever designed by Turkish engineers.

As the product range expanded, so did HİDROMEK. Today it has around 2,200 employees and builds backhoe loaders, hydraulic excavators, wheel loaders, motor graders and soil compactors.

Explore HİDROMEK
Italy

Paints & decorative coatings

Colour is not just pigment. Colour is a tool for the redevelopment of architectural space, and Loggia's projects, research and development are based on this philosophy.

Thanks to constant attention to innovation and the study of techniques for protecting both interior and exterior surfaces, Loggia has created a complete range of highly professional indoor and outdoor products, and a wide, diversified decorative line for both walls and floors.
